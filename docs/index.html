How it works

Activity becomes verified contribution.
Contribution becomes useful resources.

Community members do real work and grow. That growth is verified by trusted learning partners, anchored with KERI, and represented as Octos — a portable, verifiable record of what was actually created.

01 · ACTIVITY & GROWTH

Do the work

Verified achievements captured through the selfdrivenOS and apps like Level Up — structured with the selfdriven-initiated skillzeb Templates standard.

02 · VERIFICATION

Trusted verification

Trusted learning partners verify the work. A KERI-based representation of activity and growth underpins social and societal trust.

03 · OCTOS

Represent contribution

Octos — or OctoProtos while prototyping — are issued on verification, a portable proof of activity and growth that can be put to use.

What makes octomics work

A small set of open, verifiable components turns everyday activity into trusted, exchangeable contribution.

The Octonomous stack

Intent. Being. Contribution.

Three layers form a complete ecosystem — from why something should happen, to who carries it out, to the value it creates. Octomics is the contribution layer.

Octology The intent layer — communities and individuals express goals and desired outcomes. “What should happen?” INTENT Octonomous The being layer — self-sovereign beings subscribe to intent and act on it with character. “Who acts, and how?” BEING
Octomics The contribution layer — value, learning, and growth become visible and verifiable. “What was created?” CONTRIBUTION
